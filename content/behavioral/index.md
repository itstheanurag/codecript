Technical skills might get you the interview, but behavioral skills get you the job. Most top-tier tech companies (Google, Amazon, Meta, etc.) place a high weight on behavioral rounds to assess culture fit, leadership, and conflict resolution.

This section provides a structured approach to mastering behavioral interviews using industry-standard frameworks and detailed preparation strategies.

## What's Inside?

- **[Introduction](./001-introduction)**: understanding the purpose of behavioral interviews.
- **[The STAR Method](./002-star-method)**: A foolproof framework for structuring your answers.
- **[Common Questions & Scenarios](./003-common-questions)**: Deep dives into conflict, failure, achievement, and leadership.
- **[Questions for the Interviewer](./004-questions-for-interviewer)**: How to show interest and assess the company.

> "Prepare your stories, not your answers."
