**Algorithms** are the step-by-step recipes we use to solve problems. If Data Structures are the jars in your pantry, Algorithms are the recipes that tell you how to turn those ingredients into a gourmet meal.

This section covers the essential pattern recognition skills you need to solve complex problems and ace technical interviews.

---

## 🧭 The Pattern Roadmap

Algorithms are best learned through **Patterns**. Instead of memorizing 1,000 problems, learn these core strategic categories:

### Sorting & Searching
The bedrock of efficiency.
- [Binary Search](/algo/searching/001-binary-search)
- [Merge Sort & Quick Sort](/algo/sorting/005-merge-sort)

### Graph Traversal
Managing connections and networks.
- [BFS & DFS](/algo/graphs/012-bfs)
- [Dijkstra's Algorithm](/algo/graphs/014-dijkstras-algorithm)

### Advanced Paradigms
- **Dynamic Programming (DP)**: Solving sub-problems to find the global optimum.
- **Greedy Algorithms**: Making the local best choice at each step.
- **Backtracking**: Exploring every path and cutting off failures early.

In every algorithm guide, we analyze the **Time and Space Complexity**. We don't just care if it works—we care if it's **scalable**.
